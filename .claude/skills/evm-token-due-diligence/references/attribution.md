# Attribution discipline

- Use neutral roles until stronger evidence supports another label: **launch
  signer**, **fee recipient**, **funder**, **observed controller**, **opening
  cohort member**. Not "the team", "the scammer", "insider", "Sybil", or a
  person's name.
- These do NOT alone prove common ownership, human identity, coordination,
  fraud, or intent: shared funding, shared routers, shared exchanges, timing
  correlation, deterministic (CREATE2) deployment, common settlement
  destinations, or use of generic/public infrastructure.
- Describe observed sell-then-rebuy routing as **market-mediated
  redistribution** unless stronger claims are independently established.
- Do not call proceeds **profit** without a defensible cost basis, the material
  flows, fees, and retained inventory.
- Do not conduct broad personal-identity searches by default. Keep public
  attribution narrow and tied to authenticated project-control evidence
  (e.g., a signature from an address the project itself designates).
- Common use of a helper/disperser/airdrop contract is shared infrastructure,
  not proof the helper is project-owned.
- State the bounded proposition ("one address funded the cohort before launch")
  separately from the interpretation ("this supports prearranged
  choreography") and from what stays unproven ("one legal owner; the ultimate
  beneficiary; fraud").
